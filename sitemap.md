# Public sitemap

Only public indexable pages are listed here. Routes use the localized `/{lang}` prefix. Discovery is generated by `apps/server/src/seo.ts`; keep PUBLIC_PATHS in sync.

| Public route | Page |
|---|---|
| `/{lang}/` | Cockpit Context home |
| `/{lang}/privacy` | Operator privacy notice |

`/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml` and `/llms.txt` are Hono discovery endpoints. Authentication utilities, signed-in application pages and administrator pages are excluded from indexing.
